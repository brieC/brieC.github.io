01

Conversion Optimisation

Single-Page Checkout

A multi-step checkout flow was creating friction and cart abandonment. This redesign collapsed the entire journey into a single, scannable page to shorten the path to the "Place order" button significantly.

My role

Lead Product Designer

Focus area

UX Flow, Form Design

Tools

Figma

Type

E-commerce Web, Responsive Web

The problem

The original checkout required users to navigate through multiple separate steps, making it feel longer than it needed to. With each additional step, there was a greater chance of users abandoning their purchase.See example video of the old version of this page:

Design approach

The checkout flow was redesigned into a single-page experience, bringing shipping, payment, and order review together in one place. This gives users a clear overview of the process, reduces unnecessary navigation, and makes completing a purchase feel faster and more straightforward.

Expected outcome

A faster, more straightforward checkout experience that reduces unnecessary steps and makes it easy for users to review their information before placing an order.

02

UX Concept Design

Butter & Crumbs Mobile App

A concept mobile app created as part of the Google UX Design Professional Certificate to modernise the ordering experience for a local bakery. The project focused on replacing an inefficient phone-based ordering process with an intuitive digital solution that makes ordering simpler for customers while reducing pressure on staff.

My role

UX/UI Designer

Focus area

UX Research, User Flows, Wireframing, UI Design & Prototyping

Tools

Figma

Type

Concept Mobile App

The problem

The bakery relied on phone calls to take customer orders. As order volumes increased, the process became difficult to manage, introduced a greater risk of human error, and took valuable time away from serving customers in-store. Example screens from the final concept:

Design approach

I followed a user-centred design process, beginning with user research, personas, and user journeys before creating wireframes and interactive prototypes. The interface was designed to feel warm, familiar, and easy to navigate, allowing customers to browse products, customise orders, and schedule collection with minimal effort.


View User Personas ↗

What I learned

This project reinforced the importance of designing around real user needs rather than assumptions. It strengthened my understanding of UX research, information architecture, and translating user requirements into intuitive interfaces, while showing how thoughtful design can improve both customer and business experiences.

03

UX Redesign

How-to Videos Page Redesign

Users were struggling to find what they needed in a poorly structured video library with an endless scroll. This redesign focused on improving discoverability and reducing time-to-content through clearer information architecture and navigation patterns.

My role

Lead Product Designer

Focus area

Information Architecture, Navigation, UI

Tools

Figma

Type

Responsive Web

The problem

Users were unable to effectively navigate the original page, leading to drop-off before finding relevant help content and creating an unnecessary support burden.See example video of the old version of this page:

Design approach

Restructure the information architecture with categorised browsing to surface the most-needed content without overwhelming the user on first load.

Expected outcome

An easier-to-navigate how-to videos page that groups related content with clear categories, reducing cognitive load, and offer a direct path from question to answer.

More about
me

I'm Brizane, a Junior Product Designer (UI/UX) who started my career in front-end web development. For four years, I worked with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React.js, and Tailwind CSS, building the interfaces that users interacted with every day. Over time, I found myself becoming more interested in why those interfaces were designed the way they were and how small design decisions could shape the overall user experience. That interest led me to transition into UI/UX design. Today, I combine my technical background with a user-centred design approach to create digital experiences that balance user needs, business goals, and technical requirements.

My development experience helps me communicate effectively with engineering teams, understand implementation constraints, and design solutions that work beyond the mockup stage. I enjoy solving complex problems through thoughtful design, whether improving user journeys, refining navigation, or simplifying complex interactions.
